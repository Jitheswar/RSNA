# RSNA knee abnormality detection

## Language

**Competition objective**: First place on the final private leaderboard of the RSNA Knee Abnormality Detection competition. This is the project's ambition, not a guaranteed outcome or an achieved result.

**Zero external spend**: No additional expenditure on compute, services, data, or expert review for this effort. Existing cluster access is available subject to its queue and allocation limits.

**Reference labels**: The competition's supplied reference assessments for the twelve knee findings. Labels inferred from reports are a separate source of supervision and do not become reference labels.

**Report-derived labels**: Training targets inferred from the training reports, with potentially incorrect or missing assessments. Reports are unavailable to the model at competition inference.

**Provisional candidate**: A model with promising development results whose improvement remains uncertain. It may warrant another experiment without establishing a proven performance gain.
