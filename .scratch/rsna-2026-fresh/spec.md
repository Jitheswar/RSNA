# RSNA Knee MRI AI Challenge 2026 — Project Specification

Status: ready-for-agent

Scope: specification only. Implementation requires a separate explicit user instruction. The user's confirmation of this specification is not authorization to implement, submit jobs, upload artifacts, or select a final submission.

## Problem Statement

The project owner wants a serious competition entry and reproducible college/research project for the RSNA Knee Abnormality Detection Challenge 2026. The system must turn heterogeneous knee MRI DICOM studies into twelve study-level abnormality scores, exploit radiology reports safely during later training experiments, and deliver a valid Kaggle notebook submission.

The college's KLEF Lenovo LiCO/OpenHPC/Slurm cluster has substantial CPU capacity but heavily contended GPUs. KLEF-MASTER is a login node, the owner has no root access, and persistent storage is shared NFS. A design that discovers basic pipeline defects during GPU allocation, assumes always-available accelerators, or loses progress when jobs stop would waste the project's principal scarce resource.

Trusted supervision is exceptionally sparse: fresh metadata audits found 4,407 unique training studies, only 58 with twelve reference labels, including nine MCL positives. The remaining 4,349 studies have reports without reference targets. Reports are absent at competition inference. Validation must preserve scarce reference evidence while keeping every fold's adaptation isolated. Repeated development CV can guide model selection but cannot be presented as an untouched internal test.

Earlier specifications were reported corrupted. This specification is synthesized from the current interview, fresh audits and identified official sources. Prior corrupted specifications and prematurely written implementation are not design authorities.

## Solution

Build a Python-first, Slurm-operated MRI system through tracer-bullet delivery. The first milestone proves a correct image-only path from raw DICOM through indexing, deterministic preprocessing, hierarchical study loading, a tiny trainable model, fixed-budget training, validation, twelve scores and complete competition-format inference. It may score poorly; it must behave correctly and recover predictably.

Next establish auditable report-to-target supervision, compare it against the same gold-only image baseline under controlled conditions, and only then increase image-model capacity. Stronger imaging, report supervision, distillation and ensembles must earn their place through traceable experiments and measured inference costs.

Keep a known-good immutable submission path after the first baseline. Separate local validation, actual Kaggle verification and owner-selected final status. Make the project competition-ready before October 15, 2026, then use the remaining official competition window for optimization and submission safety. Maintain a dynamic prioritized plan rather than a rigid calendar. A research demonstration follows the working ML system.

Do not open a dedicated efficiency-prize workstream during early or middle-stage modeling; retain complete runtime, VRAM, model-size and ensemble-cost measurements so later optimization does not require redesign.

### Domain and competition contract

- A study is the prediction unit, identified by the exact field StudyInstanceUID, and contains distinct series and slices; SeriesInstanceUID identifies a series. A study identifier is not assumed to be a patient identifier. Preserve source-to-study mapping throughout processing and output.
- The required output filename is submission.csv. It contains a header and exactly thirteen columns: StudyInstanceUID first, followed by ACL, MCL, Medial Meniscus, Lateral Meniscus, Medial OA, Lateral OA, PF OA, Effusion, Synovitis, Baker's, Contusion and Fracture, with spelling, capitalization, spaces and punctuation preserved. Produce twelve finite scores in the closed interval zero to one, with every expected test StudyInstanceUID exactly once, no extras or omissions, no accidental CSV index column, and rows aligned to the expected test-study order.
- Score values express model confidence suitable for ranking. They are not clinically calibrated risks. Calibration and clinical thresholds are separate later experiments.
- Competition inference is MRI-only with permitted test metadata. Reports and report-processing dependencies belong to training/research paths and are not required by inference.
- The primary competition metric is the unweighted mean of twelve target ROC AUCs. Both CPU and GPU competition notebooks must execute with internet disabled and generate submission.csv within nine hours (32,400 seconds), including setup, data loading and all inference work. GPU notebooks are permitted; using a GPU does not extend the limit.
- Official target definitions, current full rules, resource eligibility and delivery requirements must pass their verification gates before dependent work relies on them. They are not all claimed verified merely because this specification names them. Names of targets and participant interpretations do not establish clinical-positive criteria.

### Module boundaries

- Maintain lightweight interfaces for indexing, DICOM decoding and geometry, deterministic series preprocessing and cache, study loading and aggregation, supervision resolution, training and evaluation, inference, and submission formatting.
- Decoding has no training dependency. Cache creation has no model-architecture dependency. Supervision is not embedded in the imaging Dataset. Inference does not import training orchestration.

### Data, geometry and cache

- Index metadata for the complete training corpus. Initially pixel-cache all 58 gold studies plus a representative 50–200 report-only studies spanning transfer syntaxes, dimensions, orientations, slice counts and series types. Expand after the baseline works and storage/throughput evidence justifies it.
- Preserve original source data and source-version identity. Extraction must validate paths, completeness and integrity, and must not present incomplete output as admitted data.
- Preserve anatomical plane, fluid sensitivity and fat suppression as separate descriptors. Do not assume identical meanings from equal counts. Reject metadata contradictions through documented policies rather than hidden corrections.
- Spatial ordering must follow reliable DICOM geometry. Missing or inconsistent spatial metadata rejects the series, with an explicit reason. Filename order is never spatial truth. Instance number is not an automatic fallback; any future fallback requires scoped evidence and acceptance tests.
- Retain valid diagnostic MRI series. Exclude reliably identified scouts/localizers and non-diagnostic series through documented metadata rules. Ambiguous descriptions are not grounds for arbitrary anatomical guesses.
- Deterministic preprocessing covers decoding, geometry validation and ordering, explicit intensity handling, aspect-preserving resizing and deterministic padding. Baseline parameters are one grayscale channel, 128 by 128 pixels and eight evenly spaced slices per usable series.
- Respect stored-pixel padding, modality transformations and photometric interpretation explicitly. Fix numeric normalization, interpolation, decoder and geometry policies through synthetic and real-input verification before using them in training. Constant-intensity and unsupported-input behavior must be deterministic and recorded.
- Preserve all successfully decoded, accepted, deterministically preprocessed slices in each series cache. Perform cheap model-specific sampling after caching; record selected source indices. Cache identity includes source content, materialized preprocessing configuration/version and artifact schema, not model architecture or post-cache sampling count.
- Publish caches atomically with checksums and completion records. Partial or incompatible entries are not cache hits. Each cache key has one writer; running jobs and preserved artifacts retain their inputs.
- No globally learned normalization or other dataset-fitted transformation may cross fold boundaries. Fixed series-local transforms may be cached once and shared across folds.

### Validation and supervision isolation

- Use frozen three-fold iterative/multilabel-stratified development CV initially, balancing all twelve reference targets and checking class support. Do not reserve a permanent locked holdout from the 58 gold studies. There is no untouched internal test set.
- Assign every training study immutable fold membership before report-derived supervision. Gold studies receive multilabel-aware allocation; report-only studies receive deterministic allocation, preferably stable study-identifier hashing. Preserve exact membership, generation method and artifact checksum. Additional fixed-seed repeats require justification and separately preserved manifests.
- First exhaust legitimate metadata for a reliable patient/group key. If available, respect it during allocation. If reliability cannot be established, allow study-level splitting while explicitly disclosing that repeated-patient leakage cannot be ruled out. Never invent authoritative patient mappings from names, report similarity, dates or anatomy.
- A later trustworthy patient key requires regenerated folds and invalidation of materially affected earlier comparisons. Presence of a patient field alone does not prove its reliability or cross-site scope.
- A fold's held-out studies include all their series, images, metadata and reports. Exclude them from that fold's fitting, training pseudo-labels, report-example rule/prompt development, weak-rule tuning, learned normalization, self-supervised/domain adaptation and teacher/student adaptation.
- Aggregate development CV may select architecture, loss, augmentation, preprocessing policy, supervision strategy and hyperparameters. Repeated use introduces selection bias, which must be acknowledged. Aggregate selection does not authorize incorporating held-out report content into adaptation.
- Pooled gold-reference OOF macro AUC is the primary local comparison: each gold study contributes the prediction from its held-out fold. Separately report pooled target AUCs, mean fold macro, variation, positive/negative counts and uncertainty. Undefined one-class AUC remains undefined, never an invented value or a silently reduced macro average.
- Evaluate each repeated-CV OOF set separately. Do not count repeated predictions as independent studies. Bootstrap studies or reliable patient groups where available and document undefined resamples and estimator limitations.
- A final all-data refit is a separate stage after model selection. Its prior OOF evidence describes the selection process, not untouched validation of the exact refitted weights.

### Mechanics model and training

- Use a shared small 2D slice encoder, masked mean pooling within series, mean pooling across usable series, and a linear twelve-output head. Apply sigmoid for scores; compute loss from logits.
- Masks distinguish missing and padded slices/series. Fewer than eight accepted slices use their real observations with padding rather than unmarked repeated evidence. All-invalid studies bypass model aggregation into explicit failure/fallback behavior.
- Start with masked binary cross-entropy and reference labels only. Known reference or admissible confident weak positives/negatives may later supervise corresponding entries. Unknown, uncertain, unmentioned, failed or unavailable labels have zero supervision mask.
- Normalize loss over supervised entries. Fully masked batches must not advance optimizer or scheduler progress as though learning occurred, and persistent inability to make progress must fail explicitly.
- The mechanics baseline uses fixed preprocessing, fixed initialization/seed/budget and final-checkpoint evaluation. Later early stopping or best-checkpoint selection is permitted through a consistent recorded development policy; never select epochs from Kaggle feedback.
- Previously reviewed numeric defaults include a small three-stage encoder with 8/16/32 channels, Adam with learning rate 0.001 and constant schedule, 100 successful optimizer steps per fold, batch size two, separately named seeds and float32 series caches. Treat these as provisional engineering choices, not claims of optimality; verification may motivate a documented new configuration.

### Report-derived labels and later research

- Milestone two compares an auditable deterministic extractor against a specifically approved pretrained language-model extractor. Evaluate accuracy, coverage, failure, uncertainty, auditability, repeatability and compute/latency, including negation, historical findings and absence of mention.
- No guaranteed clinical mentor is assumed. Use verified official definitions; keep ambiguous interpretations explicit and optionally reviewable. Avoid inferring clinical labels from mere mentions.
- Preserve weak-label states positive, negative, not-mentioned, uncertain and extraction-failed. Initially only admissible confident positive/negative states supervise training. Reference labels take precedence; disagreements remain auditable. Model self-confidence is not assumed to be calibrated reliability.
- Every weak-label record preserves extractor/config/version, source-report reference, fold context, confidence and rule/model evidence privately. Rules materially derived from a report are ineligible for a model whose fold holds that study out. Shared rules require origins clean for every consuming fold. Ambiguous provenance is contamination.
- Frozen extractor evaluation on held-out gold reports is evaluation-only; those outputs and report content cannot enter that fold's training adaptation. Keep evaluation artifacts separate from admitted supervision.
- Teacher, pseudo-label and student artifacts declare eligible/excluded study sets, fold, code/config, checkpoints and generation/output hashes. Anything whose isolation cannot be reconstructed is inadmissible for serious model-selection evidence.
- The first gold-only versus gold-plus-weak comparison fixes architecture, folds, preprocessing, initialization, seeds, optimizer/schedule, augmentation, batch size, optimizer-step budget, loss and evaluation. Record mixture and actual exposure counts. Focal loss, class balancing, confidence weighting and soft-label losses are separate later experiments.
- External learned resources require proposals stating exact source/version, license and competition eligibility, public-weight timing, purpose, benefit, leakage risks, reproducibility, execution location, report-transfer behavior, compute and deterministic comparator. Approval is model or tightly defined family/version specific. No external API receives competition reports by default.
- After controlled supervision evidence, consider stronger 2D/2.5D imaging and plane aggregation, then carefully selected 3D models, transformers, self-supervision, pseudo-labeling, distillation and ensembles. Each is an experimental hypothesis with a budget, comparison and inference-cost gate.

## Testing Decisions

- The user already confirmed the test seams during the interview and accepted the interface specification. This synthesis does not reopen those questions. Prefer the highest public seam: a study processed through the shared inference/submission interface produces complete, correctly mapped twelve-target output or a documented recovery result.
- A second essential high-level seam is a fixed-budget scientific run through training, interruption/resume and held-out evaluation. It must preserve scientific identity, complete OOF mapping and trustworthy supervision provenance.
- Test lower public seams only where they isolate critical behavior that is difficult to diagnose at study level: spatial ordering/decoding, atomic cache publication, fold/supervision admission, masked aggregation, checkpoint compatibility and metric undefinedness. Avoid tests coupled to private methods, internal call counts or implementation-shaped mocks.
- Public fixtures are synthetic. Private real-DICOM integration manifests select representative competition series on-cluster; real data and identifiers are not committed or required by off-cluster CI. No established prior test suite existed before this project; prematurely written tests are not accepted as authoritative prior art and require review before any future implementation reuse.
- Geometry tests cover shuffled enumeration, misleading filenames/instance numbers, absent or inconsistent orientation/position, duplicated/conflicting locations and explicitly unsupported acquisitions. A series with uncertain geometry must not silently become valid through discarded evidence.
- Pixel tests cover signed values, modality transforms, padding/ranges, constant and all-padding inputs, nonfinite data, unusual dimensions, supported polarity and decoder fallback. Compare against known fixtures and semantic expectations, not a second copy of the algorithm.
- Cache tests verify that interruption and concurrent publication cannot create a valid partial entry; changed source/materialized preprocessing invalidates affected entries, whereas sampling/model changes do not. Preserve source-index provenance.
- Study/model tests cover variable series/slice counts, short series, masks, batch padding invariance, hierarchical pooling, all-invalid input and twelve finite bounded outputs. Test finite loss/backward and learning on a deliberately tiny fixed fixture before requesting GPU training.
- Supervision tests enforce reference precedence, distinct weak states, all-masked behavior, immutable folds and rejection of artifacts whose teacher/rule/input provenance includes held-out influence or cannot prove exclusion.
- Evaluation tests use known perfect, reversed and tied rankings; verify identity joins, pooled versus fold metrics, one OOF prediction per gold study per repeat, undefined one-class cases and correct reference-only denominators.
- Recovery tests cover atomic saves, preserved previous checkpoints, complete training state, compatible resume, incompatible semantic changes, explicit forks and bounded transient retry classification. Record replayed work rather than silently counting it twice.
- On-cluster acceptance exercises actual DICOM through decoder, cache, loader, model, loss/backward, inference and submission validation. Add representative compressed encodings based on inventory; a small header-only sample cannot establish decoder coverage.

## Out of Scope

- This task does not authorize production implementation, implementation tickets, training jobs, new environment work, uploads, deployment or final competition selection. It produces and publishes the specification only.
- The mechanics milestone excludes report fusion, external pretrained weights by default, segmentation/localization, registration, learned cropping, atlas alignment, aggressive anatomical canonicalization, sophisticated attention, 3D models, transformers, ensembling and multi-GPU dependence.
- The first controlled report-supervision comparison excludes simultaneous changes to image architecture, optimization budget, focal/class-balanced/confidence-weighted loss or soft-label treatment. These may be separately proposed later.
- Paid cloud compute/APIs/storage/data/services, outside medical datasets, proprietary report APIs and arbitrary pretrained resources are excluded unless explicitly approved and competition-eligible.
- An always-available GPU assumption, login-node training, system Python/PyTorch, Docker-daemon dependency, LiCO AI Studio as the core pipeline, enterprise plugin architecture and mandatory external experiment service are excluded.
- A permanent locked holdout is deliberately excluded given the reference-label scarcity. Clinical generalization, clinical superiority, calibrated risk estimates and exact bit-for-bit GPU determinism are not promised.
- The later demo excludes public patient MRI/report uploads and clinical-service behavior. MERN, MongoDB and Express are not requirements for ML components. Any deployed demo or use of competition-derived examples needs its own rights and deployment decision.
